Cars.com AI layoff details

Reported layoffs: Not disclosed

Industry: Technology · Geography: United States

MarketWatch reported that Cars.com would reduce its full-time workforce by about 11% as part of a cost-cutting push while focusing more on AI-powered products.

MARKETWATCH / DOW JONES NEWSWIRES2026-04-09
"Simplifying our business also provides more bandwidth to dedicate to key products, like AI, data and analytics, that we believe are critical to the future of automotive retail," Chief Executive Tobi Hartmann said.
EVIDENCE: AI-ADJACENT RESTRUCTURING

Cars.com had about 1,700 full-time, part-time, seasonal, and temporary employees at the end of 2025, but the 11% cut applies to full-time workforce only, so layoffsCount remains null rather than estimating from a mixed headcount base. The company cited AI, data, and analytics as key products, but the article also frames the plan around broader cost cutting and vendor optimization.
