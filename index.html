Methodology

Entries in this dataset are sourced from primary statements or reputable reporting and labeled by AI relevance. Many AI layoffs have been questioned by economists and analysts who argue companies use "AI washing" as cover for financial restructuring and pandemic-era overhiring. That skepticism is noted where relevant. The receipts are included so you can judge for yourself.
